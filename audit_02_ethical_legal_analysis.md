The justification is that a "hard" opt-out (deleting everything instantly) would result in a broken user experience if the user changed their mind.

Ultimately, unless an "opt-out" triggers a hard delete of the underlying data indices, the system remains "aware" of the information. For many users, this makes the toggle feel like a suggestion rather than a command.
